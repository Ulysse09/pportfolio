SariNow Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SariNow is a designated driver mobile app operated by SariTech ("SariNow," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect from riders and drivers, why we collect it, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the SariNow mobile app on iOS and Android and any related services.

1. Information We Collect

SariNow collects the information described below to connect riders with drivers, verify driver identity, process payments, and keep the platform safe. We collect information you give us directly, information generated automatically as you use the app, and, for drivers, information required to verify identity and driving eligibility.

2. Account & Contact Information

When you create a SariNow account, we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, which we verify with a one-time passcode (OTP) sent by SMS
  • Your account type — rider or driver
  • If you sign in with Google, basic profile information provided by Google (such as your name and email address) via secure sign-in (OAuth)

3. Location Data

SariNow is a location-based service, so location data is central to how it works:

  • We collect your device's precise (GPS) location, while the app is open and in use, to match riders with nearby drivers, calculate routes and directions, and show accurate pickup and drop-off points, using Google Maps and Google Places.
  • We do not collect your location while the app is closed or running in the background.
  • Location permissions can be granted, changed, or withdrawn at any time in your device's Settings. If location access is denied or turned off, core features such as driver matching and navigation will not function correctly.

4. Driver Identity Verification (KYC)

To keep riders safe and comply with our obligations as a transportation platform, drivers must complete an identity verification ("KYC") process before accepting rides. As part of this process we collect:

  • Government-issued ID — photos of the front and back of a national ID or equivalent document, uploaded from your photo library
  • Driving license — a photo of your driving license
  • Selfie verification — a live photo taken with your device's front camera, used to confirm that you are the person shown on the submitted ID

Submitted documents are reviewed before a driver account is approved to accept rides. We request camera access only to capture the verification selfie, and photo library access only to select and upload the images above — the app does not access your camera or photos for any other purpose. KYC documents are stored securely and used solely for identity verification, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

5. Ride & Trip Data

When you book, accept, or complete a ride, we collect and store trip records, including:

  • Pickup and destination addresses
  • The identities of the rider and driver involved in each trip
  • Trip status (e.g., requested, in progress, completed)
  • Fare and payment details associated with the trip

This information is shared between the matched rider and driver only to the extent necessary to complete the trip (for example, so a driver can see the pickup address, or a rider can see driver arrival status).

6. Payment Information

SariNow currently supports cash payments only. Payment is made directly between the rider and driver in person — SariNow does not process, handle, or take custody of any payment, and does not collect card numbers, mobile money PINs, or any other payment credentials.

  • Once a rider confirms that cash payment has been made, we store a record of that confirmation associated with the trip: the amount, the fact that it was paid in cash, a reference number for the transaction, and its status (e.g., successful).
  • This record is used only to maintain an accurate trip and earnings history for drivers and riders, and to support customer service and dispute resolution — it is not a financial transaction processed by SariNow itself.
  • Additional payment methods (such as card, mobile money, or PayPal) may be introduced in the future. If they are, this policy will be updated before they become available to describe how those payments are handled and by whom.

7. Ratings & Reviews

After a trip, riders and drivers may rate each other and leave an optional written comment. Ratings and comments are linked to the relevant trip and to the rider and driver profiles involved, and are used to maintain service quality and platform safety.

8. Notifications

We use real-time notifications to keep riders and drivers updated on trip and payment status (for example, "driver arriving," "payment successful"). These notifications are delivered through our backend infrastructure and may include your ride status, payment status, and amount, shared only with the rider and driver involved in that specific trip.

9. Device & App Permissions

Depending on your role and how you use the app, SariNow may request the following device permissions:

  • Location (while using the app only) — for matching, navigation, and live tracking
  • Camera — for driver selfie identity verification only
  • Photo library — to upload ID and driving license images during driver verification

SariNow does not request access to your microphone or audio recording, and does not access these permissions in the background outside of the purposes described above. You can review or change any permission at any time in your device settings.

10. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and manage your rider or driver account
  • Match riders with available, verified drivers and calculate routes
  • Verify driver identity and driving eligibility before approving a driver account
  • Process and record payments for completed trips
  • Send trip, arrival, and payment status notifications
  • Maintain ratings and address safety or quality concerns
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the SariNow platform
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

11. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to operate SariNow:

  • Between matched riders and drivers — limited trip details (e.g., name, pickup/destination, driver arrival status) needed to complete a ride
  • Google — for maps, place search/autocomplete, directions, and Google sign-in
  • Our backend and infrastructure providers (including Supabase, for account, trip, payment, and KYC data storage, and Firebase, for authentication support and real-time trip notifications) — who process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations
  • Legal and safety purposes — where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of SariNow, our users, or the public

12. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored using reputable third-party infrastructure providers with industry-standard security controls, including encrypted connections and access controls that restrict who can view sensitive data such as KYC documents and payment records. Privileged actions, such as permanently deleting an account, are performed only through secured backend functions and are never handled with elevated credentials inside the mobile app itself. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

13. Data Retention

We retain account, trip, payment, and KYC information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward, where needed to resolve disputes, maintain financial and safety records, or comply with legal obligations. When you delete your account (see below), your associated payment records, ratings, KYC submissions, and trip history tied to your profile are deleted from our active systems, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.

14. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us (see below). Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law.

SariNow also supports in-app account deletion. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your payment records, ratings, driver KYC submissions, and trip history associated with your profile, and then delete your account credentials. This action is irreversible.

15. Children's Privacy

SariNow is not directed at children, and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, our practices, or legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where changes are material, we will provide additional notice through the app.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, please contact SariTech at upkabalisa@gmail.com.
