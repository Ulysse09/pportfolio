  • Drivers must hold a valid driving license and any other authorization required by applicable law to operate a vehicle, and must keep this information accurate and current.
  • Approval to drive on SariNow does not constitute an employment relationship, and drivers operate as independent providers of designated driving services.

  • SariNow does not guarantee driver availability at any given time or location.
